Privacy

Last updated 6 October 2026. This covers the Miss Minutes desktop app and this web demo.

Short version: Miss Minutes has no servers, no accounts and no analytics. Your tasks stay on your own computer. Google and AI features are off until you turn them on, and each one sends only what it needs.

What the desktop app stores, and where

Your tasks, projects, reminders and settings are kept in a database file on your computer (%APPDATA%\Miss Minutes\missminutes.db on Windows). Nobody else, including the developer, can see it. Uninstalling the app does not delete it; you can delete that folder yourself.

Google Calendar and Google Tasks (optional)

If you connect a Google account, the app talks directly from your computer to Google. There is no server in between.

Sign-in tokens are encrypted on your computer with your operating system's protection (Windows DPAPI) and are never sent anywhere except Google. You can disconnect at any time in the app's Settings, or remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Miss Minutes' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone.

AI features (optional)

AI features use Anthropic's Claude API with your own API key, which is stored encrypted on your computer. Each feature sends only the fields it needs:

  • Natural-language capture: the sentence you typed, today's date and time zone, and your project and tag names.
  • Task breakdown: the task's title, notes (you can turn notes off), project name and due date.
  • Plan my day: the candidate tasks' titles, estimates, due dates and priorities, plus your free time slots. Calendar event titles, attendees, locations and descriptions are never sent.

What Anthropic does with requests is covered by Anthropic's privacy policy. With AI turned off, nothing is sent.

This web demo

The demo runs entirely in your browser. Sample tasks and the calendar are generated on your device, and anything you add is saved in your browser's local storage. It never connects to Google or to an AI service, and nothing you enter is sent anywhere. The page is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub's own privacy statement covers what GitHub logs when serving it. Use “Start over” in the demo, or clear this site's data in your browser, to remove what you added.
